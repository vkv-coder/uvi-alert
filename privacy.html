Privacy Policy

UVI Alert is a simple reminder tool used to keep track of follow-up actions with customers and contacts, and to send them a WhatsApp reminder message.

What information is collected

For each reminder you create, the app stores: a name, an address, a contact number, a short note describing the reminder, and the date it's due for. No payment information is collected.

Why it's collected

This information is used only to show you your list of reminders by date, and to pre-fill the WhatsApp message you choose to send to that contact.

Who can access it

The app is protected by a PIN. Only someone with the correct PIN can view or add reminders. Data is stored in a private Supabase database; it is not shared with any third party.

Where it's stored and how long

Data is stored in a secure, India-region hosted Supabase database for as long as the reminder is kept in the app. Deleting a reminder removes it permanently.

WhatsApp messages

Tapping a contact's number opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. The message is only sent if you personally tap "Send" inside WhatsApp — this app never sends a message automatically.
